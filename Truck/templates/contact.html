Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions — no need to contact us for these.

How do I track my delivery in real time?
Log in to your customer portal and open the job from "My Jobs". The tracking map shows your courier's live GPS position and updates every 5 seconds with a live ETA countdown.
How is the delivery price calculated?
Pricing uses a base fare of $50.00 plus $2.50 per mile, with an 8% fuel surcharge on the mileage cost. The route distance is computed via our self-hosted OSRM road network engine — not straight-line estimates. The minimum fare is $75.00.
What areas do you currently serve?
We operate primarily in Georgia and the surrounding southeastern states including Tennessee, Kentucky, Alabama, South Carolina, and North Carolina. Our OSRM routing covers all these areas for accurate mileage pricing.
How do couriers get paid?
Couriers receive payouts directly to their PayPal account. Payments are processed by admin after the customer confirms delivery. You can set your payout email in the courier portal under "Payout Method".
My courier hasn't arrived — what should I do?
First check the live tracking map for your courier's current location. If the tracker shows no movement for over 30 minutes, use the contact form above and mark it as Urgent. We'll escalate immediately to reassign or contact your courier directly.
How do I become a verified courier?
Sign up via the Courier portal. You'll be prompted to upload your driver's licence photo. Our ML pipeline (PaddleOCR + InsightFace) verifies the document automatically. Most verifications complete in under 2 minutes. You can also upload a selfie for face verification.
